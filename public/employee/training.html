Recognizing Phishing Attacks

Phishing is a cyberattack where an attacker attempts to trick you into revealing sensitive information or clicking a malicious link.

What should you look for?

  • Unexpected or suspicious emails.
  • Urgent requests asking you to act immediately.
  • Suspicious links or unfamiliar websites.
  • Requests for passwords or sensitive information.
  • Fake login pages that imitate trusted websites.

Remember

Always verify the sender and website before clicking a link or providing sensitive information.
